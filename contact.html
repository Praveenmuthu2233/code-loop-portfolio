// Common Questions

FAQ

Are all CodeLoop tutorials really free? +
Yes — 100% free, always. Every tutorial is published on YouTube with no paywalls, no premium tiers, and no hidden courses. The mission is to make quality programming education accessible to everyone.
Which programming language should I start with? +
Python is great if you want to see results quickly and enjoy clean syntax. Java is excellent if you want to deeply understand OOP concepts. Both have complete beginner series on the channel — check the Courses page for the recommended learning path.
Can I request a specific tutorial topic? +
Absolutely. Use the contact form above or drop a comment on any YouTube video. Popular requests are prioritised — the more people ask, the sooner it gets made.
What is Interview Hub 360? +
Interview Hub 360 is a companion platform at interviewhub360.netlify.app designed to help you prepare for technical and HR interviews. It has curated question banks, topic roadmaps, and coding challenges — completely free.
How do I collaborate or sponsor content? +
Send an email to codeloop9563@gmail.com or use the contact form above with "Collaboration / Partnership" as the subject. Praveen personally reviews all collab requests.
How long does it take to get a reply? +
Typically 1–3 business days for email. Comments on YouTube videos are also monitored and answered when possible. For fastest responses, reach out via LinkedIn.
